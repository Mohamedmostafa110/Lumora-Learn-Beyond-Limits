Detailed Architectural Explanations:
Q1: PACELC Latency vs Consistency Trade-off

Under normal conditions (without network partitions), distributed systems must balance the latency of cross-node sync against strict serializable consistency.

Q2: Transactional Outbox Pattern

The Outbox pattern writes both the business entity change and the event row within the same ACID database transaction, eliminating dual-write failure states.

Q3: Idempotent Consumer Deduplication

At-least-once brokers will inevitably deliver duplicates during network reconnects or rebalances; consumers must check idempotent correlation keys.
